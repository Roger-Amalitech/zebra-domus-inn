Users

People who can sign in to this dashboard, and what each one may do.

  • Owner Records purchases, sets budgets, approves count differences, sees money reports and the audit log, changes rooms and prices.
  • Manager Checks purchases into the store, issues supplies, counts stock, handles staff requests and bookings, adds staff.
  • Staff Asks for supplies for their department and confirms receiving them. Can't change stock numbers.
