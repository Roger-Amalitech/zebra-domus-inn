  • Changes and cancellation

    Need to change or cancel? Contact us as early as possible and we'll help.

  • Paying

    When you book on this site, choose MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, card or pay at the inn. At reception we accept cash and major cards, including Visa, Mastercard and American Express.

    Children of all ages are welcome. Cribs and extra beds aren't available, so please tell us the number and ages of children when you book.

    Pets are welcome at no extra charge.

    Parties and events aren't allowed.
