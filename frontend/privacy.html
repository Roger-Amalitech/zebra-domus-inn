Privacy notice

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This notice explains what Zebra Dormus Inn does with the details you give us when you book a room on this website, by phone or at reception.

Who we are

Zebra Dormus Inn, Huye, Southern Province, Rwanda. We decide how your details are used. To ask a question about your details, use the phone, WhatsApp or email shown in the Contact section.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your stay: dates, room, number of guests and how you would like to pay.
  • Anything you tell us to help with your stay, such as a special request.

Your name, phone number and email are needed to book online, so that we can confirm your booking. If you prefer not to give them online, you can book by phone or at reception. We do not take card or mobile money details on this website.

Why we use them

  • To keep your room for you and confirm your booking.
  • To contact you about your stay, for example to arrange payment or answer a question.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records the law asks us to keep.

We do not sell your details, share them for advertising, or send you marketing messages without asking you first. No decisions about you are made automatically.

Who can see them

  • Inn staff who look after bookings. Each person signs in with their own account.
  • The companies that host this website and its booking records for us (Render and MongoDB Atlas). Their servers may be outside Rwanda. They store the details for us and may not use them for anything else.

How long we keep them

We keep booking details for as long as we need them for your stay, and afterwards only as long as the law requires for our records. Then we delete them.

How we protect them

The website uses a secure connection (https). Only signed-in staff can see bookings, and the manager or owner can turn off a staff member's access at any time.

Your rights

Under Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy, you can ask us to:

  • show you the details we hold about you;
  • correct details that are wrong;
  • delete your details, or stop or limit how we use them;
  • stop using your details for something you agreed to before.

Contact us using the details in the Contact section. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to Rwanda's National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA), which supervises data protection.

What this website keeps in your browser

The website remembers the language you choose in your own browser. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The map on our home page is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies when the map loads.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
